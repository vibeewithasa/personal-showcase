# Demonstration session assets

The four scenes in this folder were generated specifically for Personal Showcase with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-11-02. They are AI-generated demonstration artwork, not real client sessions, commissioned photography, a real photographer's portfolio or photographs obtained from third-party stock libraries.

Publication copies were encoded non-destructively as JPEG/WebP. Generated source PNGs remain outside the repository; no user/client photographs were used as references or uploaded. No people or branded objects were requested. Replace these folders with approved photographs for your own portfolio. Keep the AI credit while retaining the demonstration imagery.

## Asset and prompt set

- coastal-light/DSC_0001.JPG: horizontal fictional rocky coast, blue hour, dark basalt foreground, soft sea mist, thin warm horizon, restrained natural colours, detailed film-like texture. No people/boats/buildings/brands/text/watermarks or recognizable landmark.
- coastal-light/02 Roca fría.webp: vertical fictional isolated sea stack, sea foam, pale cloudy sky, quiet negative space and tactile rock. No people/buildings/boats/brands/text/watermarks or identified location.
- quiet-forms/01.jpg: horizontal fictional minimal concrete passage, rectangular opening and diagonal late-afternoon sunlight, mineral plaster and deep shadows. No people/furniture/signage/recognizable existing building/text/watermarks.
- quiet-forms/02.webp: vertical fictional sculptural stone stairwell, curving pale wall and overhead light, dark negative space and realistic stone texture. No people/furniture/signage/identified real building/text/watermarks.

All prompts requested one standalone editorial photograph, not a collage, as clearly labelled AI demonstration artwork for an open reusable photography portfolio.

coastal-light has optional metadata, an explicit cover, alt text/captions and both camera/Unicode filenames. quiet-forms deliberately has no session.json: its title/count/gallery are discovered without metadata.
